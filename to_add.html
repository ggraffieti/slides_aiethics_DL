--- ### The main enemy: bias > "the action of supporting or opposing a particular person or thing in an unfair way, because of allowing personal opinions to influence your judgment." - Bias is not always unwanted: - Used to perceive possible dangers by almost all animals. - Pareidolia. - Basis of Bayesian Statistics (degree of belief). - Bias may be essential to ML and humans for decision making. Note: Example of noise from bush, stick vs snake. If we were totally bias free, we would not take decision at all! We must evaluate all possible possibilities (even the most remote ones) --- ### (Bad) Biases are in everyday life!
- Beauty bias - Halo/Horns effect - Conformity bias - Status quo bias - Authority bias - Idiosyncratic bias - ...
Note: Idiosyncratic bias: occurs when managers evaluate skills they're not good at highly. Conversely, they rate others lower for skills they're great at. In other words, managers weigh their performance evaluations toward their own personal eccentricities. --- ### (Bad) Biases are in everyday life! --- ### Let's pause a bit, and think about humans now - Humans are not perfect decision making machines - Juror decision are affected by sport results - [Emotional Judges and Unlucky Juveniles](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22611?utm_campaign=ntw&utm_medium=email&utm_source=ntw) - Self-driving cars may look scary, but: - [1.19M people die every year by crashes, 1st cause of death for people under 30](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries) - What about human-AI collaboration? - What if AI is right 99.999% of the time? - What if AI is so convincing that persuade the human to take the wrong decision?
